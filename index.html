About

Coastal Corporation Ltd. engages in the aquaculture and marine food products business. The company processes and exports shrimp, fish, and seafood products to international markets including the USA, Europe, and Asia. Established in 1979, it operates processing plants across Andhra Pradesh with integrated facilities for breeding, farming, and export of value-added seafood. The company is known for its quality standards and consistent dividend payouts.

Key Points

  • Major exporter of shrimp & seafood products
  • Operates 3 processing plants in Andhra Pradesh
  • Exports to 20+ countries worldwide
  • Consistent dividend payer since 2001
  • Integrated aquaculture operations
